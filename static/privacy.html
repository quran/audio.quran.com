Quran Audio

Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026

Quran Audio is free and contains no advertising. Core listening does not require an account. The Android launch version keeps playlists, history, downloads, preferences, and Khatmah progress on your device and does not enable developer analytics, crash reporting, or session replay by default.

Quran Audio is provided by Quran Foundation, Inc. ("Quran Audio," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how the Quran Audio mobile application handles information. For privacy questions or requests, contact audio@quran.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the information needed to provide its features locally on your device. This can include downloaded recitations, playlists and favorites, recently played items, playback position, preferred reciters, appearance and playback settings, listening activity, repetition history, local search history, and Khatmah progress. You can remove supported items in the app, clear the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the app. Your operating system may include app data in a device backup when system backup is enabled.

Streaming, downloads, and ordinary network information

The app contacts Quran Audio, Quran.com, and related content-delivery services to fetch reciter catalogs, Quran text and study content, artwork, audio, verse timing, optional speech-model files, and other app content. As with any internet request, these services necessarily receive ordinary network information such as your IP address, request time, requested resource, and connection or user-agent information. This information may be used to deliver content, protect the services, diagnose failures, and prevent abuse. Online requests use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app requests microphone access only when you choose a voice-search or recitation-practice feature. When offline recognition is available, audio is processed on the device and the raw recording is not saved by Quran Audio. Depending on your device and configured speech provider, a voice feature may instead send audio to the operating system's speech-recognition service for real-time transcription. Quran Audio does not retain that raw audio; the speech provider's privacy terms and controls apply. You may decline or revoke microphone access and continue using non-voice features.

Analytics and diagnostics

The Android launch version disables Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, PostHog analytics, and PostHog session replay by default. It does not request advertising identifiers for analytics. If a future Android version offers optional diagnostics or analytics, it will require an explicit choice and this policy and the relevant store disclosure will be updated before collection begins.

On other supported platforms, limited product-interaction, crash, and performance information may be collected for analytics and diagnostics as disclosed in that platform's store privacy label. That information is not used for third-party advertising or sold.

Accounts and cloud sync

The Android launch version does not require or expose account sign-in or cloud sync. If an account or cross-device sync feature is enabled in a later release, we will update this policy, provide the required deletion controls, and update the app-store disclosures before launch.

Creating and sharing media

Verse images and videos are created on your device. They are not uploaded to a Quran Audio account simply by being created. If you choose Save, the app writes the media to your device. If you choose Share, your operating system sends the selected content to the app or recipient you select; that destination's privacy practices then apply.

Advertising, sales, and disclosures

Quran Audio does not display third-party advertisements and we do not sell personal information. We use service providers only to operate the app and deliver requested content. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users or our services, or as part of an organizational transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

Children

Quran Audio is a general-audience religious audio app. Core listening does not require an account, and the Android launch version does not enable developer analytics by default. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian may contact us to request review or deletion of information.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the app and the information it handles. Information processed by content-delivery or speech-recognition providers may be handled in countries other than your own, subject to those providers' arrangements and applicable law. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Your choices and requests

  • Use core listening without an account.
  • Decline or revoke microphone and notification permissions in device settings.
  • Delete playlists, downloads, and supported activity in the app.
  • Clear all local app data in device settings or uninstall the app.
  • Email audio@quran.com with a privacy request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required.
